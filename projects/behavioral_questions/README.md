# Behavioral Questions

## About
- A track for the non-technical half of a software engineering interview: "Tell me about a
  project you are proud of", "Tell me about a time you failed", conflict, ambiguity, ownership,
  motivation.

## Requirements
- New category `/behavioral-questions`, listed under **Software Engineering** in the site nav,
  next to System Design.
- About 10 posts: an introduction (STAR and what interviewers score), a story-bank post, then one
  post per question family.
- Sample answers are built from **Folau's real projects**, taken from the progress reports in
  this repo. Nothing is invented. Where no documented story exists, the post teaches the shape of
  the answer with a clearly-labelled template rather than a made-up story.
- Keep posts to the point.
