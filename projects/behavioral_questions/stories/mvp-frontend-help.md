Give an example of a time when you went above and beyond the requirements for a project.
Situation: In a previous role, we were working on our MVP. The project had a tight deadline and was crucial for our business. We needed to launch our MVP so we could validate some of our business assumptions. We also needed it out so we could get our seed round funding.
Task: While my primary responsibility was backend development which consisted of backend APIs and infrastructure, I noticed that our front-end team was overwhelmed and at risk of falling behind schedule, which could delay the entire project.
Action: Even though it wasn't required or expected, I offered to assist the front-end team after completing my regular backend duties. I spent extra hours in the evenings learning the necessary front-end technologies and collaborating closely with the team to implement critical features. This included optimizing code for performance, ensuring cross-browser compatibility, and conducting additional rounds of testing to guarantee a seamless user experience.
Result: My contribution helped the front-end team meet their deadlines, and we were able to deliver our MVP on time. Our CEO and CTO were extremely satisfied with the quality and performance of the application. This experience not only helped me enhance my front-end development skills but also demonstrated the importance of teamwork and going the extra mile to ensure project success.
